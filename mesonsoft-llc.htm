Mesonsoft LLC

Technology & Innovation — founded by Shiva Dhanuskodi

Mesonsoft LLC is the technology and innovation company behind a growing family of AI, sports, family, and community products. It was founded by Shiva Dhanuskodi, who also created AniShiv World. Mesonsoft is based in the Washington DC–Baltimore area. For the company's own website, visit mesonsoft.com.
